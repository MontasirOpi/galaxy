Cosmic Architecture

Anatomy of a Barred Spiral

The Milky Way is classified as a barred spiral galaxy (SBbc). From its central supermassive singularity to the ghostly dark matter envelope, each layer shapes galactic evolution.

Sagittarius A* (ধনু রাশি A*)
Sol / সূর্য (~26,000 ly)
1. Galactic Bulge & Central Bar
~27,000 ly

A dense peanut-shaped bulge of ancient Population II stars and a 27,000 light-year long stellar bar driving interstellar gas into the core engine.

2. Thin & Thick Disks
1,000 ly

The thin disk harbors young stars, open clusters, gas nebulae, and our Sun. The thick disk holds older stars oscillating hundreds of light-years above and below the plane.

3. Stellar Halo & Globulars
150+ Clusters

A vast spherical halo enclosing the disk, populated by ancient globular clusters like Omega Centauri that date back 12+ billion years.

4. Dark Matter Halo
~90% Mass

An invisible, colossal envelope reaching out to nearly 1 million light-years, providing the gravitational scaffolding that keeps outer stars revolving at high velocities.

Orion-Cygnus Arm (কালপুরুষ উপবাহু)

The Orion Arm is our galactic neighborhood. Nestled between the Sagittarius and Perseus arms, it contains the Solar System, the Orion Nebula nursery, and our local sky constellations.

DENSITY WAVE DYNAMICS (ঘনত্ব তরঙ্গ)

Stars drift into and out of spiral arms like vehicles in highway density waves. When vast hydrogen clouds compress within these waves, brilliant new starbursts ignite.

Why the Accretion Disk Forms a Halo

The gravity of Sagittarius A* curves spacetime so intensely that light from the back of the disk is bent upward over the shadow and downward underneath it. You are observing both sides of the disk simultaneously!

Gravitational Time Dilation

Near the event horizon, clocks tick much slower compared to distant observers on Earth. One hour spent in deep orbit near Sagittarius A* could equal decades or centuries passing for the rest of the universe.

The Cosmic Point of No Return

The Event Horizon is the ultimate boundary of our universe. Once matter or light slips across this threshold at 24.5 million kilometers, the escape velocity exceeds the speed of light, sealing its fate into the singularity.

Birth of Proto-Galactic Clouds

Primordial gas clumps collapse, igniting Population III stars in the ancient core.

10.0 BILLION YEARS AGO

Gaia-Enceladus Merger

A dwarf galaxy crashes into the infant Milky Way, puffing up stars into the thick disk.

4.6 BILLION YEARS AGO

Solar System Ignition (সূর্য ও পৃথিবীর জন্ম)

A molecular cloud collapses in the Orion Spur, forming the Sun and planets.

+4.5 BILLION YEARS (FUTURE)
